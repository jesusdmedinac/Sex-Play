# Setup Flow Improvements & Brainstorming

This document tracks potential feature enhancements and UX/UI ideas to improve the **Game Setup** flow for *Sex Play*.

---

## 1. 🎚️ Game Mood & Category Filters (Vibe Selection)
Allow couples to select the mood of the action deck for their session:
* **Romantic & Sensory:** Focuses on light massages, eye contact, gentle caresses, and emotional connection.
* **Playful & Dares:** Focuses on fun dares, dances, cheeky Q&As, and laughter.
* **Intense & Erotic:** Focuses on direct stimulation, high anticipation, and erotic challenges.
* **Random Deck:** Full mix of all available action cards.

---

## 2. 🚫 Safety & Hard Limits Checklist (Consent Filter)
Provide a quick consent management menu to exclude specific types of triggers or activities:
* **Temperature Play:** Exclude ice/heat elements.
* **Impact Play:** Exclude spanking or physical impact.
* **Personal Questions:** Exclude Q&A regarding past experiences.
* **Public/Outdoor Dares:** Restrict all dares exclusively to the bedroom/indoor environment.

---

## 3. 🎭 Player Roles & Custom Avatars
Allow symbolic role assignment or visual personalization:
* **Role Options:** Dominant / Submissive / Switch, or Caregiver / Pampered.
* **Visual Customization:** Distinct color themes or avatar icons representing each player during their turns.

---

## 4. ⏱️ Game Modes & Duration Limits
* **Express Mode (10 Cards):** Quick warm-up session before intimacy.
* **Standard Mode (20 Cards):** Balanced evening experience.
* **Edging Marathon (Endless):** No card limit; the game only finishes when a player surrenders via the Panic Button.

---

## 5. 🛡️ Custom Safe Word Configuration
* Allow players to input a custom **Safe Word** (e.g., *"Red"*, *"Pineapple"*).
* Display the Safe Word discreetly but persistently on the gameplay screen to reinforce consent and comfort.

---

## 6. 🪄 Wizard-Style Setup Flow (Multi-Step Stepper)
Replace the single form screen with an engaging step-by-step wizard:
* **Step 1: Players Profile:** Enter names, choose avatars/colors, and set roles (optional).
* **Step 2: Experience & Vibe:** Select game mood and maximum intensity limit.
* **Step 3: Boundaries & Safe Word:** Configure hard limits and custom safe word.
* **Step 4: Summary & Ready:** Confirm settings and launch the game.
